## Overview

*Fitness Zona Training Center* is a fitness and health website designed to provide visitors with information about fitness programs, healthy living, and the services offered by the training center.

The website was created as a web development project to practice and demonstrate different *HTML and CSS concepts*, while creating a simple and user-friendly fitness website.

The main goal is to provide visitors with a place where they can learn about Fitness Zona, explore its services, and get in touch with the training center.

---

## Features

- *Home Page* – Introduces Fitness Zona Training Center.
- *About Page* – Provides information about the training center.
- *Fitness Services* – Displays the different fitness and health services offered.
- *Sign Up Form* – Allows users to enter their information through a registration form.
- *Contact Page* – Provides contact information for visitors.
- *Navigation Bar* – Allows users to easily move between different pages.
- *Responsive Design* – Uses CSS techniques to make the website adaptable to different screen sizes.
- *CSS Styling* – Uses colors, fonts, spacing, sizing, and layout to improve the appearance of the website.

---

## Services

Fitness Zona Training Center focuses on helping people achieve their fitness and health goals through:

- *Weight Loss Journey*
- *Build Muscle*
- *Keeping Fit*
- *Healthy Diet*
